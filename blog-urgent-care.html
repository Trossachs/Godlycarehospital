When should you seek urgent medical attention?

Godly Care Specialist Hospital · Health Education

Some symptoms require prompt clinical assessment. If you are concerned about a serious or rapidly worsening condition, seek appropriate emergency medical care.

Health information should support—not replace—individual clinical assessment. If you have symptoms or concerns, speak with a qualified healthcare professional who can assess your circumstances and provide appropriate advice.
